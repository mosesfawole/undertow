# Official entry requirements

Verified 9 October 2026 from the organizer's **Unusual Whales Hackathon #1: Official Rules & Terms**, last updated 17 September 2026, attached in the pinned [Hackathon Info thread](https://discord.com/channels/710524439133028512/1525246043460079729/threads/1550233789215809687). The organizer's 8 October reminder confirms the same deadline.

- Entry period: 18 September through **23 October 2026, 11:59 p.m. Eastern**. Deadline in UTC: 24 October, 03:59; Berlin: 24 October, 05:59.
- Entrants must be at least 18 or their local age of majority, whichever is greater, have valid UW API access, and live where participation and receipt of a cryptocurrency prize are lawful. Trial keys qualify. One entry per person; original work required.
- Post in `vibe-and-api-projects`, with a `Hackathon1:` title, working demo video or images, project information, endpoints, setup instructions and GitHub repository link. Never include credentials.
- Judging: usefulness 30%, originality 20%, UW API use including MCP/agent 25%, working implementation and reproducibility 15%, presentation/documentation 10%.
- Prizes: 1,000 USDC for first place; 600 and 400 UW API credits for second and third.
- Entrants retain ownership, while granting the organizer a non-exclusive, royalty-free worldwide license to display and promote the entry and entrant name/handle in connection with the hackathon.
- Section 10 says entrants are responsible for removing publicly shared entry files after the hackathon. Monetization must wait until after the event and requires prior arrangements with UW.
- Raw UW data must not be publicly redistributed. The rules allow screenshots of one's own results; Undertow's public demonstration uses synthetic data.
- Entry accepts the full terms, including liability release, indemnity and dispute provisions. This summary does not replace the official PDF. Entrants must review it and confirm eligibility and acceptance before posting.

The entrant confirmed eligibility and acceptance of the rules before publication. [The entry was submitted](https://discord.com/channels/710524439133028512/1557955284125618266) on 9 October 2026. No judging decision or prize result is claimed.
